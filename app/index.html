How downloading works

Requests share a paced queue. Opening a work gives that work priority; it still respects any cooldown requested by the archive. Prioritise changes the order jobs are served without increasing the request rate.

Pause keeps your queue. Stop ends the job and keeps what arrived. Completed jobs stay here so you can check the result or retry missing works.

Android may suspend downloads when the app is in the background. Open Fan Folio to continue; downloaded works are kept for offline reading.

Delete this work?

The text, the pictures and your place in it all go. Fan Folio remembers that you deleted it, so a bookmark sync or an author's index will not quietly put it back — you can allow it again under Removed in settings.

Block ?

Nothing of theirs will be fetched again — not from their index, not through anybody else's bookmarks. Works with another author on them are left alone. You can unblock them in settings, but the works are not coming back.
