Keeping it safe

Everything the app holds is a single file: the works, the chapters, where you are in each of them, and the search index. A copy of it is a copy of the whole library.

Importing replaces what is here now. Back up first if this library has reading in it that the backup does not.

Archive account

Signing in lets works be fetched from the archive, including anything restricted to logged-in users.

Feedback

A small tick when a page turns, refuses to turn, or a sheet is pushed away. Your phone's own haptic setting still has the last word.
